Background & Story

About Me

I'm George, a Computer Science student who spends more time in Flutter than in lecture halls.

Right now I build mobile apps for real. Not class exercises, but a real client project, El Malky, where I worked on the parts that actually matter to a user: logging in, ordering, paying, seeing their order history, switching between Arabic and English without the app feeling like it was stitched together. That's where most of my current experience comes from, and it's the part of my work I can stand behind fully.

If you're reading this, you probably fall into one of two places. Either you have an idea and no clear way to turn it into something people can actually use, or you already have something running, and there are parts of it (or your workflow around it) that feel more manual than they should. I understand both, because building El Malky put me right next to that second problem: I watched where the manual, repetitive work sat around the app itself, not just inside it. That's what pulled me toward AI Automation, n8n, APIs, AI-powered workflows, not as a trend I picked up, but as the next real gap I noticed while doing the actual work.

I won't tell you I'm an AI engineer. I'm not, yet. What I can tell you is that I already know how to take an idea through to a working app, and I'm learning how to look past the app itself at the workflow around it because I've seen firsthand where that workflow gets heavy.

I don't just build the app. I help you think about what happens after it ships, specifically the repetitive parts that AI and automation could start taking off your plate.

If you've got an idea, or something that already exists but feels heavier to run than it should, let's talk about it.
